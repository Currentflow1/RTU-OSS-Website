Program objectives

The program develops graduates with statistical knowledge, practical skills, analytical ability, and the capacity to adapt to professional and academic environments.

  • Technologically competent and armed with practical skills congruent to the needs of the industry and the workplace in general.

  • Knowledgeable of the proper application of statistical methodology and have experience in using the computing tools for effective statistical analysis.

  • Proficient problem solvers through analysis of data.

  • Prepared to pursue graduate studies.

  • Adaptive, flexible, and driven to practice the profession.
